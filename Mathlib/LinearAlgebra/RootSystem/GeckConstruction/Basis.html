    Geck's construction is complete #

    The Geck construction of a Lie algebra associated to a root system, RootPairing.GeckConstruction.lieAlgebra, yields a simple Lie algebra. In this file we show that this construction yields all finite-dimensional simple Lie algebras up to isomorphism.

    Main definitions / results: #

    • RootPairing.GeckConstruction.basis: the Geck construction yields a basis.
    • RootPairing.GeckConstruction.equivRootSystem: up to equivalence, LieAlgebra.IsKilling.rootSystem is left inverse to RootPairing.GeckConstruction.lieAlgebra.
    • RootPairing.GeckConstruction.equivLieAlgebra: up to equivalence, LieAlgebra.IsKilling.rootSystem is right inverse to RootPairing.GeckConstruction.lieAlgebra.
