    Dimension of vector spaces #

    In this file we provide results about Module.rank and Module.finrank of vector spaces over division rings.

    Main statements #

    For vector spaces (i.e. modules over a division ring), we have

    • rank_quotient_add_rank_of_divisionRing: if V₁ is a submodule of V, then Module.rank (V/V₁) + Module.rank V₁ = Module.rank V.
    • DivisionRing.hasRankNullity: The rank-nullity theorem for division rings.
    • Submodule.rank_span_le_rank: The K-rank of the K-span of an R-submodule M of V is at most the R-rank of M.

    See also Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Dimension/ErdosKaplansky.lean for the Erdős-Kaplansky theorem.
