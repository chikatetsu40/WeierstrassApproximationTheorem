    Bilinear forms on exterior powers #

    The file is a home for results about the bilinear forms on exterior powers of a module.

    Main definitions / results: #

    • LinearMap.BilinForm.exteriorPower: a bilinear form on a module induces a bilinear form on its exterior powers.
    • LinearMap.BilinForm.bijective_exteriorPower: if a module carries a bijective bilinear form, the induced bilinear forms on its exterior powers is also bijective.
