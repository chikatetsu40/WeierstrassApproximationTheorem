    Distributions on a topological monoid #

    We show that if G is a topological monoid, then D(G, R) inherits a convolution product from G, and that if G is locally compact, this makes D(G, R) into a ring (and C(G, R) into a module over it). Moreover, if G is abelian and compact, then D(G, R) is a commutative ring.

    When G has a continuous multiplication law, we can define the convolution of a measure with a function.

          Commutativity for compact abelian G #

          We show that if G is compact, Hausdorff, and commutative, then D(G, R) is a commutative ring.

          The compactness and separation assumptions arise since the product of two measures is defined by an iterated integral over G × G; a Fubini-type theorem is needed to swap the order of the two integration variables. The Fubini theorem we use (AbstractMeasure.prodMk_eq_prodMk') requires the space to be compact and separated.
