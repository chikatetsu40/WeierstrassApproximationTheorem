    The finite adele ring of a number field #

    This file concerns the finite adele ring of a Dedekind domain R and its field of fractions under the assumption that Ring.HasFiniteQuotients R and Infinite R. Later, these results are applied to the case where K is a number field and R is 𝓞 K.

    Main definitions #

    • NumberField.FiniteAdeleRing.instNormFiniteAdeleRing : the norm on the finite adele ring.

      Norm (IsDedekindDomain.FiniteAdeleRing R K)

      The norm on the finite adele ring is the product of all the local norms. If a finite adele is a unit, then this is a finite product in disguise. Otherwise, it is zero (and not the junk tprod value of 1).
