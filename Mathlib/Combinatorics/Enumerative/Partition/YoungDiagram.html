    Young diagram of a partition #

    This file defines the equivalence between Young diagrams μ with cardinality n and partitions of n, where each row of the diagram becomes a part of the partition. The two directions are YoungDiagram.toPartition and YoungDiagram.ofPartition.

    Using this equivalence, the conjugate of a partition is also defined. Given the Young diagram μ representing a partition λ, its conjugate λ' is the partition obtained by flipping μ about the main diagonal, i.e. the columns of μ are the parts of λ'. This is implemented here directly via YoungDiagram.transpose.
