    Quadrifunctors obtained by composition of multifunctors #

    Given a bifunctor F : C₁ ⥤ C₂₃₄ ⥤ E and a trifunctor G : C₂ ⥤ C₃ ⥤ C₄ ⥤ C₂₃₄, we define the quadrifunctor trifunctorComp₂₃₄ F G : C₁ ⥤ C₂ ⥤ C₃ ⥤ C₄ ⥤ E.

    Similarly, given a trifunctor F : C₁ ⥤ C₂ ⥤ C₃₄ ⥤ E and a bifunctor G : C₃ ⥤ C₄ ⥤ C₃₄, we define the quadrifunctor trifunctorComp₃₄ F G : C₁ ⥤ C₂ ⥤ C₃ ⥤ C₄ ⥤ E.
