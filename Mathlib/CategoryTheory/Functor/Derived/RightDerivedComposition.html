    Comparison morphism for the composition of right derived functors #

         F₁₂  F₂₃
  C₁ ⥤ C₂ = C₃
L₁|  L₂|   L₃|
  v    v     v
  D₁ ⥤ D₂ ⥤ D₃
   RF₁₂   RF₂₃

    In the situation above, when we have natural transformations α₁₂ : F₁₂ ⋙ L₂ ⟶ L₁ ⋙ RF₁₂, α₂₃ : F₂₃ ⋙ L₃ ⟶ L₂ ⋙ RF₂₃, an isomorphism e : F₁₂ ⋙ F₂₃ ≅ F₁₃ where F₁₃ : C₁ ⥤ C₃, and a natural transformation α₁₃ : F₁₃ ⋙ L₃ ⟶ L₁ ⋙ RF₁₃ which makes RF₁₃ the derived functor of of F₁₃ ⋙ L₃ (with respect to W₁ : MorphismProperty C₁), we define a natural transformation RF₁₃ ⟶ RF₁₂ ⋙ RF₂₃ (see Functor.natTransOfIsRightDerivedFunctorComp). We may say that this is the comparison morphism for the composition of right derived functors, even though the definition only assumes that RF₁₃ is a right derived functor. (In applications, both RF₁₂ and RF₂₃ would usually be derived functors as well.)
