    Right derived functors are triangulated #

    Let F : C ⥤ D, L : C ⥤ H, F' : H ⥤ D be functors between pretriangulated categories. Let α : F ⟶ L ⋙ F' be a natural transformation. We show that F' is triangulated if F, L, F' and α commute with shifts, F and L are triangulated, and for any morphism f in H, there exists a distinguished triangle T in C such that Arrow.mk (L.map T.mor₁) ≅ Arrow.mk f, and α.app T.obj₁, α.app T.obj₂, and α.app T.obj₃ are isomorphisms.
