    Currying of functors in four variables #

    We study the equivalence of categories currying₄ : (C₁ ⥤ C₂ ⥤ C₃ ⥤ C₄ ⥤ E) ≌ C₁ × C₂ × C₃ × C₄ ⥤ E.

    The equivalence of categories (C₁ ⥤ C₂ ⥤ C₃ ⥤ C₄ ⥤ E) ≌ C₁ × C₂ × C₃ × C₄ ⥤ E given by the curryfication of functors in four variables.
