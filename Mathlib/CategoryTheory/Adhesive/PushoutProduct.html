    Pushout-products in adhesive categories #

    This file proves that the pushout-product of monomorphisms in an adhesive cartesian monoidal category is a monomorphism.

    Mono sq.ι

    The induced pushout map (Leibniz pushout) of an F.PushoutObjObj is a monomorphism if its naturality square is a pullback and the morphisms being pulled back are monomorphisms.
