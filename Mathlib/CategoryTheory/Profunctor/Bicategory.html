    The Profunctor Bicategory #

    This file defines the bicategory ProfCat whose objects are categories and whose 1-morphisms are profunctors. The 2-morphisms are natural transformations between profunctors.

    The bicategory instance is defined on ProfCat.{u, u}, with profunctors valued in Type u. Its operations simplify to the corresponding operations in the Profunctor namespace.
