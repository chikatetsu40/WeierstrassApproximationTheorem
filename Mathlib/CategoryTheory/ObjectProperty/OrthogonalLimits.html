    Orthogonals are closed under (co)limits, quotients and subobjects #

    Let P be a property of objects in a category with zero morphisms. We show that the left orthogonal P.leftOrthogonal is closed under quotients and under colimits of any shape, and, dually, that the right orthogonal P.rightOrthogonal is closed under subobjects and under limits of any shape.

    These are registered as instances of IsClosedUnderQuotients, IsClosedUnderColimitsOfShape, IsClosedUnderSubobjects and IsClosedUnderLimitsOfShape. Closure of the orthogonals under extensions is shown in Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Abelian/OrthogonalClosed.lean; together these form the easy direction of [S. E. Dickson][dickson1966]'s characterisation of torsion classes, see CategoryTheory.Abelian.isTorsionClass_iff.

    References #

    • [S. E. Dickson, A torsion theory for Abelian categories][dickson1966]
