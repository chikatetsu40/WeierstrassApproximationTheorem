    Evaluating symmetric polynomials #

    Main declarations #

    • pow_smul_sum_map_aroots_aeval_mem_range_algebraMap: Given k a multiple of p.leadingCoeff and e ≥ q.natDegree, k ^ e • ∑ i ∈ p.aroots A, q.aeval i lies in the base ring.
    • MvPolynomial.symmetricSubalgebra.aevalMultiset evaluates a symmetric polynomial at the elements of a multiset.
    • MvPolynomial.symmetricSubalgebra.sumPolynomial maps X to ∑ i, X i.

    These are used in the proof of Lindemann-Weierstrass.
