    The category of C^n manifolds modeled on I #

    This file defines the bundled category ModelWithCorners.MfldCat.{u} I n of C^n manifolds modeled on a fixed I : ModelWithCorners 𝕜 E H, along with the forgetful functor to TopCat.

    Future work #

    • Show that ModelWithCorners.MfldCat I n has coproducts given by disjoint unions.
