    South and north poles of simplices. #

    This file defines the south and (generalized) north poles of a simplex, with respect to a vertex of that simplex: the second intersection with the circumsphere of a line through that vertex and the incenter or an excenter.

    Main definitions #

    • Affine.Simplex.nsPole for the second intersection with the circumsphere of a line through a vertex and the excenter with the given set of indices
    • Affine.Simplex.southPole for the second intersection with the circumsphere of a line through a vertex and the incenter
    • Affine.Triangle.northPole for the second intersection with the circumsphere of a line through a vertex and an excenter other than the one opposite that vertex

    References #

    • Paul Averous, Problèmes du Club de Maths
