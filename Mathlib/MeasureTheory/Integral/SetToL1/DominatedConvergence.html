    Convergence and measurability for set-to-function extensions #

    This file proves norm estimates and dominated-convergence results for MeasureTheory.setToFun. It includes sequential and filter versions of dominated convergence, applications to infinite sums, strong measurability for parameterized families, and continuity results for families dominated by an integrable function.
