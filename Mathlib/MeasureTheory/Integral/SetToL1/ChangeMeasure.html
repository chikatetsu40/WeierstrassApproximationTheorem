    Change of measure for set-to-function extensions #

    This file develops compatibility of MeasureTheory.setToFun with measurable maps and changes of measure. It first proves approximation results using integrable simple functions, then compares setToFun across dominated measures and establishes formulas for sums and scalar multiples of measures.
