    noncomputable def AddSubgroup.zmultiplesEquivInt {A : Type u_2} [AddGroup A] [IsAddTorsionFree A] {a : A} (ha : a ≠ 0) :

    The subgroup generated by a nonzero element a of a torsion-free additive group is isomorphic to ℤ, sending n • a to n.
