    Quadratic algebras and quadratic extensions #

    This file relates the concrete construction QuadraticAlgebra R a b to the predicate Algebra.IsQuadraticExtension: a QuadraticAlgebra is a quadratic extension of R, and conversely every commutative quadratic extension is isomorphic to a QuadraticAlgebra.

    Main results #

    • a QuadraticAlgebra is a quadratic extension, as an instance;
    • Algebra.IsQuadraticExtension.exists_algEquiv_quadraticAlgebra: every commutative quadratic extension is isomorphic to some QuadraticAlgebra R a b.
