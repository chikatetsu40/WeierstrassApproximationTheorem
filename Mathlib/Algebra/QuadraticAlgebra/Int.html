    Quadratic algebras over ℤ #

    For a b : ℤ, QuadraticAlgebra ℤ a b is an order in QuadraticAlgebra ℚ a b.

    Main results #

    • QuadraticAlgebra ℚ a b is the localization of QuadraticAlgebra ℤ a b at the nonzero integers and its fraction ring.
    • QuadraticAlgebra.Int.isDomain_iff: QuadraticAlgebra ℤ a b is an integral domain iff discr a b is not a square.
