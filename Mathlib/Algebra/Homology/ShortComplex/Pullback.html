    Pulling back a short complex #

    Given a short complex S and a morphism f : Y ⟶ S.X₃, we construct the short complex S.pull f, which is S.X₁ ⟶ pullback f S.g ⟶ Y, and show that it is short exact when S is, if the ambient category is abelian.

    This is dual to ShortComplex.push, see Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Pushout.lean.

    The pullback of a short complex S along a morphism f : Y ⟶ S.X₃: the short complex S.X₁ ⟶ pullback f S.g ⟶ Y, whose first map is induced by 0 and S.f and whose second map is pullback.fst.
