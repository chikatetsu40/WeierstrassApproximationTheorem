    Pushing out a short complex #

    Given a short complex S and a morphism f : S.X₁ ⟶ Y, we construct the short complex S.push f, which is Y ⟶ pushout S.f f ⟶ S.X₃, and show that it is short exact when S is, if the ambient category is abelian.

    This is dual to ShortComplex.pull, see Mathlib/Algebra/Homology/ShortComplex/Pullback.lean.

    The pushout of a short complex S along a morphism f : S.X₁ ⟶ Y: the short complex Y ⟶ pushout S.f f ⟶ S.X₃, whose first map is pushout.inr and whose second map is induced by S.g and 0.
