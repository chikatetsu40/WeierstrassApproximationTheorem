    Homotopy equivalences between chain complexes #

    If 0 ⟶ K₁ ⟶ K₂ ⟶ K₃ ⟶ 0 is a degreewise short exact sequence of chain complexes, we show that K₁ ⟶ K₂ is a homotopy equivalence iff K₃ is contractible, and K₂ ⟶ K₃ is a homotopy equivalence iff K₁.
