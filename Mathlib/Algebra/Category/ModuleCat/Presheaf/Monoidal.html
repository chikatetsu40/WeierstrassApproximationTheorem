    The monoidal category structure on presheaves of modules #

    Given a presheaf of commutative rings R : Cᵒᵖ ⥤ CommRingCat, we construct the monoidal category structure on the category of presheaves of modules PresheafOfModulesOfCommRing R. The tensor product M₁ ⊗ M₂ is defined as the presheaf of modules which sends X : Cᵒᵖ to M₁.obj X ⊗ M₂.obj X.

    Notes #

    This contribution was created as part of the AIM workshop "Formalizing algebraic geometry" in June 2024.
