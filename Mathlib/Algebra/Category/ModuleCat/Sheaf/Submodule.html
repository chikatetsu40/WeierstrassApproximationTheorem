    Submodules of sheaves of modules #

    Given a sheaf of modules M, a SheafOfModules.Submodule M is a submodule N of its underlying presheaf of modules whose membership condition is local.

    Main definitions #

    • SheafOfModules.Submodule: a submodule of (the underlying presheaf of modules of) a sheaf of modules whose membership is local.
    • SheafOfModules.Submodule.toSheafOfModules: the associated sheaf of modules.
