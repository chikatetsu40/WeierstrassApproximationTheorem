    Orthogonal complements of submodules #

    In this file, the orthogonal complement of a submodule K is defined, and basic API established. We make duplicates for Submodule and ClosedSubmodule. Some of the more subtle results about the orthogonal complement are delayed to Mathlib/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Projection/.

    See also BilinForm.orthogonal for orthogonality with respect to a general bilinear form.

    Notation #

    The orthogonal complement of a submodule K is denoted by Kᗮ.

    The proposition that two submodules are orthogonal, Submodule.IsOrtho, is denoted by U ⟂ V. Note this is not the same unicode symbol as ⊥ (Bot).
