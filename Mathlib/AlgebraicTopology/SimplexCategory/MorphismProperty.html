    Properties of morphisms in the simplex category #

    In this file, we show that morphisms in the simplex category are generated by faces and degeneracies. This is stated by saying that if W : MorphismProperty SimplexCategory is multiplicative, and contains faces and degeneracies, then W = ⊤. We first prove the stamentes in SimplexCategory.Truncated d, and then in SimplexCategory
