    Composition of edges in quasicategories #

    In this file, we define the notions of left and right homotopies on edges of simplicial sets, and we show that if X is a quasicategory, then edges can be composed (see SSet.Edge.comp and SSet.Edge.compStruct) and that this composition satisfies some form of associativity. The results are deduced from similar results for 2-truncated quasicategories.
