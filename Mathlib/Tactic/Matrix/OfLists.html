    Matrices from lists of rows #

    ofLists reads a list of rows as a Matrix, and the results here transport the list operations to the matrix ones.

    Main results #

    • ofLists_transpose
    • ofLists_mul

    Implementation notes #

    The definitions recurse on the dimensions, so on literals they reduce in the kernel to the vecCons form of the !![…] notation, and a literal in that notation is definitionally an ofLists term.
