    The ℤ√d model for the Bareiss elimination #

    The computable model of the quadratic extensions ℤ√d. The elimination runs on Expr representing ⟨a, b⟩ literals with raw integer components. Accepted entries are ⟨a, b⟩ literals, √d, or integers evaluated from norm_num.
